# House Music Subgenres and Micro-Niches

This is a practical taxonomy of house music niches and sub-niches. It is intentionally broad: some names are formal marketplace categories, some are historical club scenes, and some are DJ/producer micro-labels. House genres overlap heavily, so treat this as a listening and production map rather than a set of hard borders.

## Research Notes

Core cross-checks:

- AllMusic lists house styles such as acid house, ambient house, Chicago house, deep house, French house, garage, gqom, jazz-house, juke/footwork, left-field house, microhouse, progressive house, tech-house, tribal house, and UK garage.
- Beatport-style taxonomy separates modern DJ-market categories such as afro house, bass house, deep house, funky house, jackin house, melodic house and techno, minimal/deep tech, organic house, progressive house, tech house, and house.
- Academic and dataset work on fine-grained house classification commonly distinguishes modern festival/streaming categories such as future house, bass house, progressive house, and melodic house.
- Regional scenes matter: South African house, kwaito, gqom, amapiano, UK garage, Baltimore/Jersey/Philly club, Chicago juke/footwork, and Latin/Brazilian variants often sit partly inside and partly adjacent to house.

## Quick House DNA

Most house music is built from these shared ingredients:

- 4/4 kick-drum pulse, usually around 115-130 BPM, though related scenes stretch slower or faster.
- Repeating bassline, often syncopated against the kick.
- Offbeat open hats, claps or snares on beats 2 and 4, percussion loops, and groove-based drum programming.
- Chord stabs, pads, organ, piano, strings, disco samples, soul/R&B vocals, or synth hooks.
- DJ-friendly arrangement with 8/16/32-bar phrasing, intro/outro drums, breakdowns, and gradual filter/FX movement.

## Parent Niches and Sub-Niches

### 1. Classic, Chicago, and Jacking House

Core elements: raw drum-machine grooves, jacking basslines, simple hooks, repetitive vocal chops, piano/organ stabs, and a physical club feel rooted in Chicago and post-disco dance culture.

Sub-niches:

- **Chicago house**: TR-707/TR-909-style drums, jack rhythm, simple bass sequences, gospel/disco/soul DNA, stripped club arrangements.
- **Classic house**: umbrella for 1980s-1990s house vocabulary; piano riffs, diva vocals, organ bass, bright snares, and clear verse/breakdown/drop structure.
- **Jacking house**: exaggerated swing and body-movement groove; punchy drums, short vocal shouts, walking bass, minimal harmonic development.
- **Ghetto house**: raw Chicago house with faster tempos, explicit party chants, minimal synth/bass patterns, and repetitive call-and-response vocals.
- **Booty house**: ghetto house adjacent; sexually direct chants, sparse drums, Miami bass/club influence, fast and functional DJ tools.
- **Hard house, US style**: tougher 1990s club house; pounding kick, hoover-like synths, diva samples, rave stabs, and aggressive builds.
- **Warehouse house**: less a strict genre than a production aesthetic; raw room tone, long drum sections, acid/deep/disco influences, and minimal polish.
- **Piano house**: prominent piano riffs, uplifting chord progressions, diva/gospel vocals, strong hands-in-the-air breakdowns.
- **Organ house**: Hammond/Korg M1-style organ bass and chords, gospel/soul phrasing, compact hooks, and rolling drums.
- **Vocal house**: song-led house centered on full vocals, hooks, backing harmonies, and club mixes.
- **Diva house**: big belted vocals, theatrical breakdowns, dramatic piano/strings, often close to garage and soulful house.

### 2. Acid House

Core elements: Roland TB-303-style resonant basslines, squelchy filter movement, repetitive hypnotic patterns, and machine-funk drums.

Sub-niches:

- **Chicago acid house**: stripped drum machines, 303 bass, raw mixer moves, minimal arrangement.
- **UK acid house**: rave-era energy, brighter percussion, psychedelic samples, smiley-era warehouse feel.
- **Acid jack**: jacking Chicago groove plus 303 riffing; funkier than acid techno and more body-focused.
- **Deep acid house**: 303 lines softened with deep chords, pads, and warm bass.
- **Acid deep tech**: low-lit minimal/deep-tech drums with restrained acid modulation.
- **Acid tech house**: tech-house drum weight and DJ-friendly drops with 303 hooks.
- **Acid tribal house**: layered percussion and 303 motifs, often darker and more hypnotic.
- **Acid progressive house**: long-form builds, evolving 303 sequences, melodic pads, and atmospheric breakdowns.
- **Acid breaks house**: house tempo and acid bass with breakbeat drum sections.
- **Psychedelic acid house**: heavy delay, flanging, vocal fragments, drones, and extended trance-like repetition.

### 3. Deep House

Core elements: warm chords, soulful/jazzy harmony, understated drums, subby bass, pads, restraint, and emotional atmosphere rather than obvious drops.

Sub-niches:

- **Chicago deep house**: raw but soulful; jazz/soul chords, drum-machine swing, compact basslines.
- **New York deep house**: garage/soul influence, polished drums, vocal hooks, and nightclub warmth.
- **Detroit deep house**: techno-adjacent, machine soul, lush chords, broken/abstract edges.
- **Jazzy deep house**: Rhodes, seventh/ninth chords, walking bass, live-feeling percussion, jazz samples.
- **Soulful deep house**: gospel/R&B vocals, warm keys, organic bass, and smooth club arrangements.
- **Dub deep house**: dub chords, tape delay, echo sends, sparse vocals, spacious low end.
- **Ambient deep house**: soft kicks, wide pads, atmospheric textures, low-intensity groove.
- **Hypnotic deep house**: long loops, subtle modulation, restrained percussion, meditative repetition.
- **Deep tech house**: deeper bass and darker room tone than mainstream tech house; minimal hooks, low-slung groove.
- **Deep minimal house**: stripped motifs, clipped drums, small chord fragments, and subtle swing.
- **Deep disco house**: disco loops filtered into a warmer, lower-pressure deep house frame.
- **Deep tribal house**: deep chords plus hand percussion, polyrhythms, and darker drum circles.
- **Deep Afro house**: Afro-house percussion and vocals with deep-house pads/chords.
- **Deep progressive house**: smooth long-form builds, deep bass, melodic pads, restrained peaks.
- **Deep melodic house**: emotional arps and pads with deep-house drum softness.
- **Lo-fi deep house**: tape noise, muffled drums, dusty samples, warm but intentionally degraded.
- **Outsider deep house**: rough, experimental, sometimes noisy or ambient-informed deep house.
- **Float house**: very soft, airy, pad-heavy deep/lo-fi house; drifting and low-impact.
- **Aquatic house**: watery pads, liquid FM keys, soft percussion, often deep/ambient-adjacent.

### 4. Soulful, Gospel, and Garage House

Core elements: songcraft, singers, church/R&B harmony, swing, live-feeling keys, and emotional release.

Sub-niches:

- **Soulful house**: full vocal performances, gospel/R&B chords, warm bass, smooth percussion.
- **Gospel house**: choir vocals, organ/piano, call-and-response, church harmony, uplift.
- **Garage house, US/New York**: soulful vocals, swing, organ bass, club-ready extended mixes, deeper groove.
- **Jersey garage**: New Jersey club lineage; soulful vocals, punchy drums, organ/piano riffs.
- **Speed garage**: UK garage offshoot with heavier bass, shuffling drums, and house/garage swing.
- **2-step garage**: broken kick patterns, syncopated snares, chopped R&B vocals, sub bass.
- **4x4 UK garage**: straight house kick with UKG swing, bass pressure, and chopped vocals.
- **Todd Edwards-style cut-up garage**: micro-chopped vocal mosaics, bright chords, tight swing.
- **Vocal garage**: song-forward UK/US garage with clear hooks and polished vocals.
- **Deep garage**: garage swing and vocals with deeper pads, warmer chords, and less pop structure.
- **Bassline house**: UK bass-heavy garage/house; wobble bass, cheeky hooks, high-energy drops.
- **Organ bassline house**: M1/organ riffs, bouncy bassline movement, shuffling drums.
- **Niche/bassline, UK**: Sheffield/UK club sound; warped bass riffs, 4x4 drums, MC-friendly space.

### 5. Disco, Funky, Filter, and French House

Core elements: disco/funk samples, loops, filters, compression, bright drums, guitar/bass riffs, and celebratory dancefloor energy.

Sub-niches:

- **Disco house**: disco loops, strings, guitar licks, live bass, filtered breakdowns.
- **Funky house**: funk basslines, brass stabs, guitar chops, vocal hooks, upbeat drums.
- **French house**: heavy sidechain compression, filtered disco samples, glossy synths, punchy kicks.
- **Filter house**: filter sweeps as the main drama; loop-based builds, sample drops, compressed drums.
- **French touch**: broader French house aesthetic; disco, electro, pop hooks, talkbox/vocoder, sleek compression.
- **Nu-disco house**: modern disco production with house drums, polished bass, synth strings, clean mixdowns.
- **Indie dance house**: house tempo with indie/electroclash guitars, synth-pop bass, and band-like hooks.
- **Boogie house**: boogie/funk bass, 1980s synth chords, slap bass or synth bass, smooth vocal chops.
- **Italo house**: Italian piano/disco/Euro influence, bright keys, romantic chords, melodic bass.
- **Balearic house**: sunlit, eclectic, slower or mid-tempo, acoustic guitars, pads, Latin/disco/ambient influences.
- **Tropical disco house**: steel-drum/marimba-style hooks, bright percussion, beach-club mood.
- **Sample house**: sample collage approach; recognizable loops reshaped into DJ tools.
- **Edit house**: disco edits tightened with house kicks and DJ-friendly structure.
- **Jackin disco house**: disco samples with jacking drums and heavier swing.

### 6. Tech House, Minimal, and Deep Tech

Core elements: house groove with techno sound design, dry drums, rolling bass, short vocal chops, club-function structure, and precise low-end movement.

Sub-niches:

- **Tech house**: punchy kick, rolling bass, sparse synth hooks, percussive loops, functional drops.
- **Minimal tech house**: fewer parts, micro-edits, tight percussion, dry bass, small sound-design changes.
- **Minimal/deep tech**: deeper, groovier, less explosive than tech house; subby bass, reduced vocals, after-hours mood.
- **Deep tech**: dark room tone, dubby stabs, minimal vocals, rolling low end, subtle pads.
- **Romanian minimal / Rominimal**: long, patient grooves, tiny percussive details, elastic bass, hypnotic repetition.
- **Microhouse**: minimal house with clicks, pops, tiny samples, glitch detail, and soft funk.
- **Click house**: microhouse-adjacent; percussion made from clicks, snaps, glitches, and granular artifacts.
- **Dub tech house**: dub chords and delay sends over tech-house drums.
- **Dark tech house**: heavier bass, minor-key stabs, ominous FX, fewer vocals.
- **Peak-time tech house**: bigger drops, louder drums, obvious hooks, festival/large-room utility.
- **Latin tech house**: tech-house drums with Latin percussion, Spanish vocals, montuno-like riffs.
- **Tribal tech house**: congas, toms, chants, and polyrhythms within tech-house structure.
- **Acid tech house**: 303 lines, resonant bass hooks, tech-house drums.
- **Industrial tech house**: metallic hits, warehouse reverb, distorted percussion, austere bass.
- **Garage tech house**: swung garage vocal chops and bass movement in a tech-house frame.
- **Minimal funk house**: clipped funk samples, small bass riffs, dry drums, understated groove.

### 7. Progressive, Melodic, and Organic House

Core elements: long arrangements, gradual builds, melodic motifs, atmospheric pads, evolving automation, and emotional tension/release.

Sub-niches:

- **Progressive house, classic/underground**: long-form tension, hypnotic arps, smooth bass, gradual filter and arrangement changes.
- **Progressive house, festival/EDM**: bright supersaws, big breakdowns, anthemic chords, vocal hooks, large drops.
- **Melodic house**: emotional chord progressions, arps, warm bass, polished drums, less aggressive than melodic techno.
- **Melodic house and techno**: shared category where house grooves meet techno atmospheres, arps, and darker synths.
- **Organic house**: acoustic/ethnic instruments, soft drums, natural textures, meditative mood.
- **Organic house/downtempo**: slower, deeper, less club-driven; hand percussion, flutes, strings, kalimba, field recordings.
- **Desert house / playa tech**: Burning Man-adjacent organic/progressive sound; dusty percussion, tribal/folk samples, expansive builds.
- **Ethnic deep house**: deep/organic house using regional instruments, modal melodies, and global percussion.
- **Afro melodic house**: Afro percussion and vocal phrasing with melodic-house arps and pads.
- **Cinematic house**: film-score pads/strings, dramatic breakdowns, spacious drums, narrative builds.
- **Emotive progressive house**: sentimental leads, lush pads, long breakdowns, restrained drops.
- **Hypnotic progressive house**: fewer big hooks, more repetition, deep bass, evolving percussion.
- **Trance-influenced progressive house**: saw leads, gated pads, long risers, euphoric breakdowns without full trance tempo.
- **Deep progressive breaks house**: progressive-house atmosphere with breaks or hybrid broken-beat sections.

### 8. Electro, Complextro, Big-Room, and Festival House

Core elements: aggressive synth design, obvious drops, compressed drums, bright toplines, and high-energy festival arrangement.

Sub-niches:

- **Electro house**: distorted saw bass, punchy kick, big synth riffs, sharp drops.
- **Complextro**: rapidly changing bass/synth timbres, chopped edits, dense fills, glitchy drop programming.
- **Big-room house**: minimal breakdown-to-drop formula, huge kick, simple lead hook, wide festival reverb.
- **Festival house**: umbrella for mainstage EDM house; vocal builds, supersaws, explosive drops.
- **Dutch house / dirty Dutch**: squeaky high synth leads, tribal-ish drums, sharp snare builds, bouncy rhythm.
- **Melbourne bounce**: offbeat bass, bouncy kick/bass pattern, pitched vocal chops, party leads.
- **Bounce house**: broader festival bounce sound; simple melodic hooks, elastic bass, upbeat drops.
- **Future rave**: darker mainstage house/trance/techno hybrid; big leads, minor keys, rave stabs, huge drops.
- **Rave house**: old-school rave stabs, breakbeat fills, hoovers, piano riffs, euphoric samples.
- **Mainstage progressive house**: festival progressive with pop vocals and supersaw drops.
- **Pop house**: radio-friendly song structure, clean vocals, short drops, simplified groove.
- **Slap house**: pitched-down vocal hooks, Brazilian-bass-like pluck bass, pop arrangement, darker club sheen.
- **Brazilian bass**: deep, rubbery pluck bass, sparse vocals, mid-tempo club-pop feel.

### 9. Future House and Bass House

Core elements: modern bass design, clipped vocals, plucky synths, tight drops, and EDM-era mix density.

Sub-niches:

- **Future house**: metallic/plucky bass, shuffled house groove, chopped vocals, bright short drops.
- **Deep future house**: future-house bass palette with deeper pads and smoother energy.
- **Future bounce**: bouncy bass rhythm, playful leads, crisp drums, festival-friendly hooks.
- **Future garage house**: future-house polish with UKG swing and vocal chops.
- **Bass house**: heavy bass sound design, house tempo, aggressive drops, electro/garage/bass music influence.
- **UK bass house**: bassline/UKG influence, wobble bass, shuffled percussion, MC-friendly space.
- **G-house**: gangsta rap samples/vocals, bass-house drops, dark swagger, minimal melodies.
- **Night bass**: label/scene-associated UK bass, garage, bassline, and house hybrid; dark, wobbly, club-focused.
- **Speed house**: faster bass/future-house variant, usually around 135+ BPM, ravey energy and compressed drops.
- **Confession-style bass house**: dark, clean, punchy bass-house sound with sparse vocals and sharp drops.
- **Jauz-style bass house**: dubstep/brostep-influenced bass design over house drums.
- **Future bass house**: future-bass chords and vocal chops with house drums and drop structure.
- **Wobble house**: bass house focused on modulated wobble basslines.
- **Fidget house**: late-2000s cut-up electro/bass house; wonky bass, chopped vocals, noisy edits.
- **Blog house**: electro/fidget/indie-dance era sound; compressed, distorted, punky club energy.

### 10. Tribal, Afro, Latin, and Global House

Core elements: percussion-led grooves, polyrhythms, chants/vocals, regional instruments, call-and-response, and groove over chord complexity.

Sub-niches:

- **Tribal house**: congas, bongos, toms, chants, polyrhythms, minimal harmonic content.
- **Afro house**: South African and broader African rhythmic language, deep/tribal/soulful house blend, African-language vocals, shakers, log drums or percussion.
- **Afro tech**: Afro-house rhythm with darker tech/techno synths, tougher kick, more mechanical drive.
- **Afro deep house**: deep chords, warm pads, Afro percussion, soulful vocals.
- **Afro soulful house**: gospel/soul vocals and keys with African percussion and phrasing.
- **Afro tribal house**: more drum-led and chant-focused; less melodic, more ritual club energy.
- **3-step**: South African house-related rhythm with three-step pulse feel, Afro-tech/deep-house connection.
- **Kwaito house**: slowed house grooves, township vocal style, bass-heavy loops, hip-hop/reggae influence.
- **Amapiano**: South African house/kwaito/deep-house hybrid; log drum bass, piano chords, shakers, jazzy/soulful melodies, spacious groove.
- **Private school amapiano**: smoother, jazzier, lounge/deep-house leaning amapiano.
- **Sgija**: harder, more percussive amapiano with aggressive log drum patterns.
- **Quantum sound / quantum amapiano**: newer hyperactive amapiano-adjacent style; busy drums, playful edits, dense log-drum movement.
- **Gqom**: Durban-origin house-adjacent style; raw minimal drums, broken/rolling patterns, heavy bass, dark repetition.
- **Gqom tech**: gqom rhythm with techno/tech-house synth pressure.
- **Gqom 2.0**: slower or hybridized gqom with amapiano/afrohouse elements.
- **Sgubhu**: South African club/house-adjacent bass pressure; repetitive and percussive.
- **Latin house**: Latin percussion, Spanish vocals, piano montunos, horns, salsa/merengue/cumbia influence.
- **Salsa house**: salsa horns, piano, clave feel, vocal calls, house kick.
- **Cumbia house**: cumbia rhythm and accordion/percussion motifs with house structure.
- **Bossa house**: bossa nova chords, acoustic guitar, soft percussion, lounge/deep-house feel.
- **Brazilian house**: Brazilian percussion/vocals, samba/bossa/funk influence, sunny chord language.
- **Baile house**: baile funk vocal/percussion energy mapped into house tempo and drops.
- **Moombahton house**: dembow/reggaeton rhythm influence with house synths, usually slower than standard house.
- **Arabian/Oriental house**: Middle Eastern scales, oud/ney-like leads, hand percussion, deep/organic house frame.
- **Indian/Bollywood house**: tabla/dhol/percussion, filmi vocals, sitar-like motifs, house drums.
- **Anatolian house**: Turkish/Anatolian folk instruments and scales with organic/deep/progressive house.
- **Flamenco house**: palmas, nylon guitar, Spanish vocals, Latin/deep-house rhythm.
- **Balkan house**: brass/folk melodies, odd-mode hooks, high-energy percussion.

### 11. Ambient, Leftfield, Outsider, and Lo-Fi House

Core elements: texture over polish, experimental sound sources, degraded samples, ambient pads, unusual structure, and less conventional club pressure.

Sub-niches:

- **Ambient house**: house rhythm softened by ambient pads, field recordings, and chill-out pacing.
- **Balearic ambient house**: Ibiza/chill-out influence, slower tempo, acoustic textures, sun-drenched pads.
- **Leftfield house**: unconventional rhythms, odd samples, experimental arrangement, genre ambiguity.
- **Outsider house**: raw, lo-fi, ambient/noise/techno-informed house outside polished club conventions.
- **Raw house**: rough drums, saturated samples, minimal mixing polish, hardware jam feel.
- **Lo-fi house**: vinyl noise, tape hiss, muffled drums, nostalgic chords, simple loops.
- **Vapor house**: vaporwave aesthetics, slowed samples, soft pads, nostalgic digital artifacts, house pulse.
- **Mallsoft house**: vapor/ambient mall textures with house drums, intentionally surreal or faded.
- **Hypnagogic house**: dreamy, memory-like samples, soft-focus synths, unstable nostalgia.
- **Noise house**: harsh textures, distortion, abrasive loops, house pulse under noise layers.
- **Industrial house**: metallic percussion, machine samples, distortion, dark warehouses.
- **EBM house**: body-music basslines, shouted vocals, arpeggiated synths, strict drums.
- **Darkroom house**: low-light, late-night, minimal/industrial/tech influence; restrained but tense.
- **Drone house**: sustained tones, minimal chord changes, slow evolution over a house pulse.
- **Experimental house**: irregular structures, unusual meters or sound design, deconstructed club logic.
- **Deconstructed house**: house signifiers fragmented into broken drums, abrupt edits, and abstract club forms.
- **Post-club house**: club rhythm referenced through experimental sound design, often less DJ-functional.

### 12. Jazz, Broken Beat, and Lounge House

Core elements: jazz harmony, syncopation, live-instrument feel, broken rhythms, and listening-room sophistication.

Sub-niches:

- **Jazz-house**: jazz samples or live keys/horns, extended chords, upright/electric bass feel, house drums.
- **Deep jazz house**: softer, deeper jazz-house with warm pads and understated drums.
- **Acid jazz house**: acid jazz/funk/soul instrumentation over house tempo.
- **Broken beat house**: syncopated drums, West London bruk influence, jazz/funk chords.
- **Nu-jazz house**: electronic jazz textures, live instrumentation, deep-house structure.
- **Lounge house**: smooth, background-friendly, polished percussion, soft vocals, jazzy chords.
- **Chill house**: relaxed house tempo, soft drums, gentle hooks, warm pads.
- **Cafe del Mar/Balearic lounge house**: sunset pads, acoustic guitar, world percussion, downtempo-house overlap.
- **Sophisti-house**: polished pop/soul/jazz-house lineage; elegant chords, clean vocals, refined production.
- **Swing house / electro swing house**: swing-era samples, brass, walking bass feel, house kick and drops.
- **Funk-jazz house**: funk bass and jazz chords, horn stabs, syncopated drums.

### 13. Hard, Rave, and High-Energy House

Core elements: harder kicks, faster tempos, rave stabs, aggressive bass, big builds, and maximal dancefloor pressure.

Sub-niches:

- **Hard house, UK**: fast, bouncy, hard kicks, hoovers, offbeat bass, rave breakdowns.
- **NRG house / hard NRG**: driving bass, high tempo, trancey leads, relentless energy.
- **Hardbag**: 1990s UK sound between house, handbag house, and hard dance; big diva samples and rave synths.
- **Handbag house**: camp, vocal, pop-remix-friendly house; diva hooks, piano riffs, accessible energy.
- **Donk house**: bouncy offbeat bass/donk sound, fast tempo, Northern UK club lineage.
- **Scouse house**: Liverpool/North West UK hard-bouncy house; donk bass, fast drums, party vocals.
- **Makina-house hybrids**: Spanish/UK fast rave energy with house-like structures.
- **Rave piano house**: breakbeat/rave stabs and piano riffs with house kick.
- **Old-school rave house**: warehouse samples, stabs, break fills, hoovers, crowd noise.
- **Euro house**: 1990s European pop/club house; rap verses, diva choruses, bright synths.
- **Eurodance house**: pop song structure, supersaw/string synths, 4/4 kick, rap/vocal hook alternation.
- **Happy house**: upbeat, major-key, playful hooks, bright piano, often pop-facing.

### 14. Club, Regional, and Footwork-Adjacent House

Core elements: local dance cultures, chopped vocals, fast edits, battle/dance-floor utility, and heavy regional drum languages.

Sub-niches:

- **Juke**: Chicago ghetto-house evolution; fast tempo, repetitive chopped vocals, sparse drums, dance-battle energy.
- **Footwork**: faster, more syncopated juke offshoot; complex drum programming, sub bass, vocal cut-ups, battle structure.
- **Juke house**: house-oriented juke, keeping more 4/4 pulse and club mix structure.
- **Ghetto tech**: Detroit electro/techno/house hybrid; explicit vocals, fast drums, electro bass.
- **Booty bass house**: Miami bass/booty/ghetto-house fusion, party chants, low-end focus.
- **Baltimore club house**: breakbeat club rhythm, chopped callouts, house samples, high repetition.
- **Jersey club house**: triplet-kick club pattern, vocal chops, bed squeaks/callouts, house/R&B sampling.
- **Philly club house**: Jersey/Baltimore-related bounce, chopped vocals, fast percussion.
- **Bmore deep house hybrids**: Baltimore breaks with deep-house chords and smoother bass.
- **Vogue/ballroom house**: dramatic crashes, "Ha" samples, cunty chants, runway/battle structure, Masters at Work/ballroom lineage.
- **Cunty house**: ballroom/vogue energy, sassy vocal chops, hard drums, dramatic stabs.
- **Bitch tracks**: ballroom/club tools with commanding vocal phrases and stripped percussion.
- **Jersey garage house**: Jersey vocal/garage warmth with club percussion influence.

### 15. Pop, Commercial, and Crossover House

Core elements: short arrangements, radio vocals, simplified drops, polished mixes, and streaming-friendly hooks.

Sub-niches:

- **Commercial house**: accessible vocals, clean drops, simple chord progressions, radio/playlist structure.
- **Dance-pop house**: pop songwriting over house drums; verse/pre/drop, polished toplines.
- **Tropical house**: marimba/flute/pluck leads, soft drums, sunny chords, pop vocals.
- **Chill tropical house**: lower energy, soft plucks, relaxed percussion, beach/lounge mood.
- **Pop deep house**: deep-house signifiers simplified for radio; pluck bass, smooth vocals, sparse drops.
- **Pop tech house**: tech-house groove with obvious pop vocal hook and short arrangement.
- **Cover house**: house remakes of familiar songs, often streaming/playlist oriented.
- **Vocal slap house**: pop vocal plus slap/Brazilian-bass drop.
- **Country house**: country vocal/guitar motifs with house kick and pop-dance structure.
- **Afrobeats house**: Afrobeats rhythm/vocals with house kick and club arrangement.
- **Reggaeton house**: dembow and Latin-pop vocals with house tempo/drop design.
- **K-pop/J-pop house**: idol-pop structure with house drums, future-house or deep-house production.

### 16. Dub, Techno, and Machine-Soul Hybrids

Core elements: dub delay, techno synth design, machine percussion, sparse vocals, and less overt song structure.

Sub-niches:

- **Dub house**: dub chords, echo, tape delay, spacious percussion, sub bass.
- **Dub techno house**: dub-techno chords with house swing and warmer drums.
- **Detroit house**: techno and house crossover; soulful chords, machine rhythms, futuristic mood.
- **Tech-soul house**: techno sound palette with soulful chord progressions and house groove.
- **Minimal dub house**: sparse drums, repeated dub stabs, low-end restraint.
- **Warehouse dub house**: raw room reverbs, echo-heavy stabs, stripped DJ-tool arrangements.
- **Electro-house, old-school meaning**: electro-funk/house fusion, syncopated bass, vocoder, 808-style drums.
- **Electroclash house**: punky synth-pop vocals, electro bass, house/disco rhythm.
- **Synthwave house**: retro 1980s synth palette with house groove.
- **Italo-disco house**: Italo arps, romantic synth leads, disco bass, house kick.
