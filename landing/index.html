Why Vibelton

The Ableton chat assistant that doesn't sound like AI.

Most AI music tools render audio you can't edit, or stamp out generic MIDI that screams "preset." Vibelton sits next to Live, generates editable MIDI in the styles you actually produce in, and gets out of the way.

Editable MIDI

You own every note.

No rendered audio, no stems-only outputs. Drums, chords, basslines and melodies land as MIDI clips in your project. Quantise, transpose, rewrite — it's yours.

Genre DNA

Faithful to the genre, not the algorithm.

Sixteen styles with their own swing, microtiming, bass register, chord voicings and lead phrasing. UK Garage actually shuffles. DnB actually rolls. Trap actually triplets.

Two modes

Idea Engine and The Finisher.

Idea Engine builds the foundation: tracks, palettes, chord progressions, drum loops, full sketches. The Finisher mutates what you've got — humanise, ghost notes, sibling variations, subtractive arrangements.

Local first

Runs on your machine.

A small Python server and a Live Remote Script. Your prompts and your MIDI never have to leave the studio. Bring your own OpenAI key for fancier planning, or keep it deterministic and offline.

Humanised

Groove, not grid.

Per-genre swing, weak-beat velocity curves, ghost notes, bar-to-bar variation, fills on the right boundaries. Built on the strategies in Dennis DeSantis's Making Music and decades of producer YouTube.

Open

No subscription required.

Vibelton is being built in the open. The bridge is yours, the MIDI library is yours, the prompt library is yours. Pricing, when it lands, will be one-time and honest.

Beta is coming

Snowball from zero.

One email when the public beta opens. No drip campaigns, no "limited spots", no countdown timers. Just a working build and a release note.
